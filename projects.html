Polyscope — iOS App

2026 – Present

React Native · TypeScript · Supabase · Deno · Fly.io · Claude API

  • Built and shipped a full-stack iOS app that tracks Polymarket prediction-market “whale” wallets in real time — a live trade feed, high-conviction signal alerts, a whale leaderboard, and a Claude-powered AI trade advisor.
  • Architected an always-on Deno polling worker on Fly.io that queries Polymarket's public APIs every ~5 seconds and fans trade data to clients via Supabase Realtime and Postgres with row-level security.
  • Integrated Clerk authentication (Sign in with Apple/Google, biometrics), push notifications, analytics, and crash monitoring; automated builds and App Store releases with EAS CI/CD.
  • Published on the App Store in the Finance category; free to download with a $5.99/month Pro tier.
